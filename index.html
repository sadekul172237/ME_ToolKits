Formula Library

1. Torque
T = (60 × P) / (2 × π × N)
T = Torque (Nm), P = Power (W), N = RPM
2. Mechanical Power
P = (2 × π × T × N) / 60
P = Power (W)
3. Rotational Speed
N = (60 × P) / (2 × π × T)
4. Efficiency
η = (Output Power / Input Power) × 100%
5. Energy Consumption
E = Power (kW) × Time (Hours)
E = Energy (kWh)
